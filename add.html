WD Add

A small separate app that only adds notes to a WordDrill queue: copy text anywhere, tap the WD Add icon, tap 📋 Paste, pick the area and Save.

iPhone:

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add.
  3. Open WD Add from the Home Screen and sign in to Dropbox once (each Home Screen app signs in on its own).

Computer (Chrome): use the install icon at the right of the address bar to get a small WD Add window.
